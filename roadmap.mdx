# Feature Availability

Availability can depend on your selected Agent, account, company, browser, provider keys, or connected services.

## Available

- Sign-in and three-step onboarding
- Dashboard, Chat, saved Threads, and scheduled Tasks
- Agent creation from scratch or from a template
- Agent editing for Basics, Goals, Abilities, Model, and Team
- Resources, layered memories, and conversation files
- Knowledge domains with documents, entities, relationships, and graph views
- Training sessions built from example conversations
- Workflow creation, publishing, triggers, and test runs
- App connections, including remote MCP servers
- Channels for B-Bot Chat, Embed, API, Tasks, and public templates
- Companies, invitations, provider keys, profile settings, notifications, news, and guided tours

## Context-dependent

- **Selected agent:** Resources, Knowledge, Training, Apps, Channels, Threads, and Tasks require a saved agent. They are unavailable when the built-in B-Bot assistant is selected.
- **Provider keys and services:** Model choices, AI-generated setup, image generation, app catalogs, training, and some chat tools depend on configured providers and available platform services.
- **Company access:** Shared agents, company keys, seats, and company-only chats depend on company membership and permissions.
- **Browser support:** Voice, notifications, clipboard actions, and some media behavior depend on browser permissions and supported browser APIs.
- **Deployment configuration:** Billing, analytics, web push, third-party integrations, and workspace previews can report that they are unavailable when their backing service is not configured.

## Coming soon

The channel picker explicitly marks these channel types as coming soon:

- Twilio
- n8n
- Zapier

No release date is published for coming-soon features. Features not listed here should not be assumed to be available.
