## Welcome to B-Bot Workshops

Our comprehensive workshop series will guide you through every aspect of the B-Bot platform. Whether you're a complete beginner or an experienced developer, these hands-on workshops will help you master creating, training, and deploying AI experts.

<Info>
  **Workshop Philosophy:** Each workshop is designed to be completed in 30-60 minutes with practical exercises. You'll build real experts and gain hands-on experience.
</Info>

## Learning Path

Choose your path based on your goals:

### 🎯 Quick Start Path (1.5 hours)
For those who want to get started quickly:
1. Workshop 1: Getting Started
2. Workshop 2: Your First Expert
3. Workshop 3: Chat Mastery

### 🔬 Developer Path (3 hours)
For developers building integrations:
1. Complete Quick Start Path
2. Workshop 6: Apps & Integrations
3. Workshop 7: Distribution

### 🧠 AI Expert Path (4 hours)
For those who want to master AI training:
1. Complete Quick Start Path
2. Workshop 4: Training & Fine-Tuning
3. Workshop 5: DeepAgents

### 🏆 Master Path (Full Series)
Complete all workshops to become a B-Bot expert!

## Prerequisites

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Technical Requirements">
    - Modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
    - Stable internet connection
    - Microphone (optional, for voice features)
    - Webcam (optional, for profile pictures)
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Account Requirements">
    - B-Bot Hub account (free tier available)
    - API keys for your preferred AI providers (optional)
    - Basic understanding of AI/chatbots (helpful but not required)
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Time Investment">
    - Each workshop: 30-60 minutes
    - Complete series: 6-8 hours
    - Practice exercises: Additional time as needed
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
