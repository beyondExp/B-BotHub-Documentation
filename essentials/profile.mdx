# Profile

The Profile page shows the identity connected to your B-Bot Hub account.

## Name and email

Your name and email address are read-only in the Hub. They are managed by your identity provider.

If either value is incorrect, update it through your identity provider or contact the administrator responsible for your account.

## Profile image

Select your current avatar to open the image picker. You can choose one of three sources:

- **Upload**: Select an image file from your device.
- **Generate**: Enter a description, choose a style, and generate an image.
- **URL**: Enter a direct image URL.

Review the preview, then select **Save** to attach the image to your profile.

For details about the shared image picker, see [AI image generation](/essentials/ai-image-generation).

## Related settings

Theme, language, and web push controls are on the [Settings page](/essentials/settings). Provider and B-Bot API keys are managed separately under [Keys](/essentials/provider-keys).
