# Welcome to B-Bot Hub

B-Bot Hub is the web application for creating AI agents, talking with them, and managing the resources and services they use.

<Note>
  Older screens, APIs, and documentation may use **Expert** for the same
  concept. In the current Hub, the primary UI term is **Agent**.
</Note>

## Start here

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in and complete onboarding">
    Continue through sign-in, then provide your workspace context, choose a
    first outcome, and review your launch plan. If sign-in started from a
    protected Hub page, the Hub returns you to that page afterward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who you are working with">
    Use the agent selector beneath the B-Bot Hub logo. Choose **B-Bot** for
    platform help, or select one of your agents for agent-specific work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Chat">
    Select **Chat** in the sidebar, enter a message, and start your first
    conversation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Find your way around

The sidebar groups pages by purpose:

- **Platform:** Dashboard, Chat, Threads, and Tasks
- **Build:** Agents, Resources, Training, Workflows, Apps, and Channels
- **Discover:** News and Guided tours

The user menu at the bottom of the sidebar contains Profile, Company, Settings, Keys, Billing, theme controls, and Sign out.

Collapse the sidebar when you need more working space.

Some pages depend on the selected agent or on services configured for your account. See [Feature availability](/roadmap) for details.
